Builders for a post-carbon neighborhood

Root & Ridge started as a blower-door obsessed crew and grew into full-home delivery for clients who want measurable performance, not greenwashing.

Why performance first

Tight envelopes and balanced ventilation reduce loads so heat pumps stay right-sized and operating costs stay predictable for decades.

We track embodied carbon where clients ask for it, and we default to low-VOC assemblies that keep indoor air gentle on kids and pets.
